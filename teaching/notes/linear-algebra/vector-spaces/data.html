Section 2.1: Intro to Vector Spaces

The first six sections of the course are almost completely self-contained. We understand vectors as collections of numbers or points in , and matrices as functions between them, with one particular use being to take a vector of variables to the left sides of a system of linear equations. Linear algebra is a tight-knit subject.
