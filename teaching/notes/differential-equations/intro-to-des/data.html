Definition: ODEs and PDEs

An ordinary differential equation, or ODE, is a differential equation involving a function of a single variable . In contrast, a partial differential equation, or PDE, is one involving a function of multiple variables, like , and its partial derivatives.

PDEs are substantially more complicated than ODEs — in fact, they have entire classes devoted to them — and we’ll be sticking exclusively to ODEs in this course. We’ll have more classifying definitions in the future, but we only need two more for now.
