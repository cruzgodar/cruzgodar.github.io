Section 4.3: Phase Portraits and Stability

For the final topic of the course, we’ll explore nonlinear systems of DEs. Much of our understanding of these more general systems will hinge on vector fields, and so we’ll first formalize a few notions we’ve seen in the previous sections.

Definition: sources, sinks, saddles, and centers

In a vector field, a critical point is a point for which there is no motion. If the field around exclusively flows away from it, then is called a source. If the surrounding field exclusively flows toward , it’s called a sink. If part of the field flows toward the point and some flows away, it’s called a saddle point. Finally, if the field simply spirals around the point and none flows toward or away, it’s called a center.
