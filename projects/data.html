Projects

The wide-ranging (and often niche) hobby projects I’ve found time for, outside of my public-facing work on this site. As with everything else, feel free to send me feedback on any of them!

Wilson: Applets Made Easy

Wilson is the TypeScript and JavaScript library that powers every applet on this site. It handles tasks like panning and zooming, makes parallelized GPU-based applets as easy as writing a shader, provides a robust and customizable fullscreen toolkit, and even lets you easily run 3D applets in a VR headset. All of this streamlines your work and simplifies your applets, letting you focus on the code that matters.
